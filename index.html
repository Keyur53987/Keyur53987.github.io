Research Project

Options Hedging Environment for RL-Based Strategy Development

Developing strategies for real-time, profitable options trading using deep reinforcement learning integrated with live market feeds.

  • Built a simulation environment on real NSE spot and option data, training Maskable PPO, PPO, and A2C agents to trade and dynamically hedge multi-leg straddle and strangle strategies on the NIFTY50 index.
  • Engineered a 69-feature observation space from option Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Vanna, Volga via Black-Scholes) and volatility estimators (Parkinson, Garman-Klass), with discrete action masking to filter out invalid trades.
  • Designed a multi-horizon, risk-averse reward function evaluated across 1-step and 30-step windows to discourage drawdowns and stabilize policy learning.
